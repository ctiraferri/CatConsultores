01 / The shift What a client actually buys Results as a service

A dashboard is not a result. It is a screen where a result can be seen.

The day a dashboard is handed over, the client has not gained anything yet. They gain on Monday morning, when someone looks at a number that did not exist before and changes a decision. That Monday is the product. Everything else, the data model, the measures, the integration, the server, the maintenance, is the factory: necessary, invisible and ours.

02 / What it is The definition, in plain words RaaS · Results as a Service

What Results as a Service is.

Results as a Service · RaaS

A model where what you contract is not a tool or a project with a closing date, but a concrete business result, sustained over time. Choosing the technology, building it, running it and keeping it alive stays on our side.

In Spanish we call it Resultados como Servicio. The acronym was coined by analogy with SaaS, PaaS and IaaS, the “as a service” family of the software world, and it moves the point of sale one step up: from the program to the effect of the program.

SaaS rents you the car. Classic consulting builds it to measure and hands you the keys. RaaS drives you to the destination.

The difference is not the technology. It is where responsibility ends.

09 / The limits What RaaS is not Said before we start

What it is not.

A new category is useful if it clarifies. If it also over-promises, it stops being useful.

Not · 01

Software with a new name.

If the product was already built and the only new thing is the brochure, that is SaaS with marketing. Here the result is defined first and only then is the tool chosen. Sometimes the tool already exists and nothing needs building: that is a valid answer too.

Not · 02

A replacement for your team.

The decision still belongs to the people who know the business. We take on giving them something to decide with, and training them so they do not depend on us to read it.

Not · 03

The right shape for every request.

Some jobs are a closed project and it is perfectly fine that they are: a website, a migration, a one-off development. When that is the case we say so and work it as a project, without dressing it up as something else.

10 / FAQ What we get asked most Buenos Aires · Argentina

Frequently asked questions about RaaS.

What is RaaS or Results as a Service?
Results as a Service (RaaS) is a model where what you contract is not a tool or a project with a closing date, but a concrete business result, sustained over time. Choosing the technology, building it, running it and keeping it alive stays on the provider’s side. The acronym was coined by analogy with SaaS, PaaS and IaaS, and it moves the point of sale one step up: from the program to the effect of the program.
What is the difference between SaaS and RaaS?
SaaS gives you access to a program that already exists: learning it, configuring it, feeding it and getting value from it is still your job, and that is where most of the real effort sits. RaaS takes on that whole chain and what it delivers is the result. Put shortly: SaaS rents you the car, RaaS drives you to the destination.
How is it different from traditional consulting?
Classic consulting sells hours and ends on delivery day. Your business does not: six months later the tool describes a company that has already changed. Under Results as a Service there is no closing date, because the job is for the result to hold and to keep adjusting as the business changes.
Which technologies do you use?
Power BI for dashboards, data modelling and DAX measures; integrations with ERP, point of sale and ecommerce; and custom development in React, Next.js, TypeScript, Node, Python, .NET, PostgreSQL and MySQL. We integrate more than twelve data sources: Excel, SQL Server, Google Analytics, Meta Ads, SharePoint, Google Sheets, REST APIs and more.
How long until it is up and running?
Two to four weeks to the first result in production. We work with functioning deliveries from the first week, not a single handover at the end of the project. After that the work continues: the last phase of the process is the one that never closes.
Do you only work in the City of Buenos Aires?
We are based in Caballito, City of Buenos Aires, and we work with companies across the city, the province of Buenos Aires and the rest of Argentina. Much of the work is remote, so distance is not a limitation: discovery and training sessions happen in person or over video call, whichever suits.
Is this for small companies or only for large ones?
Both. Some solutions pay off more in a single company and others across branch or franchise networks. What decides the fit is not size but whether there is a concrete business question without an answer today, or a manual process repeated every single day.
